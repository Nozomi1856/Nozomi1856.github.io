NAG-ME-QD: Neural Architecture Generation with MAP-Elites

💡 What It Does

Describe your desired neural network in natural language, and NAG-ME-QD automatically generates optimized architectures—no deep ML expertise required. The system discovers not just one solution, but a diverse archive of high-performing networks suited for different use cases.


Applications: Medical imaging, autonomous vehicles, scientific computing, and democratizing AI development for everyone.

🛠️ Technical Highlights

  • Multi-paradigm search: CNNs, Transformers, and Recurrent networks
  • Three evaluation modes: Zero-shot proxies, minimal training, and full training
  • Zero-shot metrics: SynFlow, NASWOT, Jacob_cov, Zen-Score
  • Text-to-architecture encoder for natural language interface
  • Smart mutation with Thompson sampling and curriculum learning
